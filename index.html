How SafeGuard-AI Operates

A three-step architecture engineered to integrate with existing site cameras without complex rewiring.

01

Observe

Capture or ingest supported camera and sensor data from fixed site masts, crane-mounted units, and perimeter surveillance feeds.

02

Analyze

Apply the available computer-vision models and monitoring logic to detect PPE anomalies, harness disconnections, and exclusion zone breaches.

03

Respond

Review safety events and alerts through the monitoring dashboard, generating instantaneous supervisor dispatch and OSHA-compliant event ledgers.
